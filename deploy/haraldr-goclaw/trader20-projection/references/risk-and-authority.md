# Risk and authority boundary

Candidate, tree, policy, plan, and account hashes are bindings, not permission. A plan approval, model statement, natural signal, status response, or open-order readback is never an operator receipt.

The operator lane requires a cryptographically verified authority envelope and fresh single-use receipts. The envelope binds candidate commit/tree, account fingerprint, policy hash, authenticated principal, client, origin, owner-sender hash, nonce, expiry, maximum intent count, maximum gross notional, and an exact 5 bps execution cap. PLAN and EXECUTE receipts use separate nonces and exact binding hashes.

The control service preserves the policy's trade, campaign, cluster, daily-loss, KILL, position-count, leverage, initial-margin, gross-margin, liquidity, concentration, protection, and pending-effect gates. Every transformed size and the final writer seam recheck current limits. Receipt and authority expiry remain blocking through provider submission.

Neither GoClaw nor Hermes projection contains a signer, exchange credential, wallet mutation, transfer, withdrawal, broad shell, or raw exchange-write operation. They submit typed requests only to the incumbent Trader20 control service. Missing verifier, authority, transport, freshness, source integrity, protection evidence, or one-writer proof blocks new effects while owned exits/protection remain governed by their explicit authority.
