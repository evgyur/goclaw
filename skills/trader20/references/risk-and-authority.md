# Risk and authority boundary

The Release-A skill has no money authority. It does not hold account credentials, signing keys, wallet access, exchange-write methods, production mounts, or mutable trading state.

Candidate and policy hashes are evidence bindings, not permission to trade. A natural-signal authorization, model statement, plan, status response, or open-order readback is never an execution receipt.

Preserve exact identifiers and provider-returned instrument coverage, including HIP-3. Fail closed on malformed identifiers, absent bindings, stale evidence, unavailable provider readback, account ambiguity, or unsupported claims. Never conflate Pear, a Hyperliquid master account, Privy, a leader wallet, or an agent wallet.
